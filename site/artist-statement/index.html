Artist Statement

My studio practice is project-based, concept-driven, and participatory. Interdisciplinary and non-media speciﬁc in nature, I mainly work with technology, imaging, performance, and writing as my tools for expression. Drawing diverse schools of eastern philosophy from my own cultural heritage, and hosting them in modern conversations with science and technology, I examine pattern, perception, and the tensions of communication in contemporary life. My art constructs precise conditions in which natural and cultural forces unfold, often among and around the public — valuing clarity of intent while leaving space for contingency, discovery, and reflection.
